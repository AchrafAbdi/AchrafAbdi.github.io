About Me

Welcome to my homepage, where I share a selection of projects I have worked on throughout my engineering journey. I hope you enjoy exploring them and find them interesting.

My engineering background began in automotive engineering, where I developed a solid understanding of internal combustion engines, vehicle diagnostics, fault detection and mechanical design using CAD.

I continued my academic journey in Mechatronics at Bochum University of Applied Sciences, where I learned the fundamentals of object-oriented programming and gained experience with Java, C and particularly Python, while expanding my knowledge of electromobility and sustainable development.

Through various practical projects, I further developed my skills in industrial automation, robotics, embedded systems and the integration of hardware and software to create practical engineering solutions.
